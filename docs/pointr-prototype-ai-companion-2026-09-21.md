# The prototype's AI companion, driven: the Assistant, its states, its answers — 21 September 2026

The round AI button beside the prototype's search field (`https://agentic-search-zeta.vercel.app`,
the Mobile SDK tab) was the one flow the two earlier reports left aside
([pointr-prototype-screen-states-2026-09-20.md](pointr-prototype-screen-states-2026-09-20.md) §8,
[pointr-prototype-initial-sheet-2026-09-20.md](pointr-prototype-initial-sheet-2026-09-20.md) §3).
This is that flow, **driven, not eyeballed**: a Playwright script
(`scripts/measure-prototype-ai.cjs`) taps the button, types four requests and two follow-ups,
runs the "Voice input", taps a suggested card, closes and reopens the chat, and opens the Filters
panel with and without the chat over it; after every step it reads the prototype's React state
(`mChatOpen`, `chatReveal`, `chatPuck`, `chatThinking`, `chatListening`, `chatDraft`,
`chatMsgs`, `filters`, `mCat`, `mPoi`, `mDetent`), the chat page's boxes and computed styles, the
AI ring's computed animation, the sheet's result rows, the map's pins, and what
`elementFromPoint` finds over the Filters button. The rules came from the bundle
(`index-DNiyTJIJ.js`, its `chatIntent`, `openChat`, `closeChat`, `sendText`, `micTap`) and the
stylesheet (`index-DPMhTnK7.css`, the `aiSpin` / `aiWave` / `aiPulse` keyframes); the script
writes both out as `bundle.json`. Numbers are CSS pixels as `(x, y, w, h)` from the top-left of the
402 × 874 phone frame; colours are the computed values; four runs gave the same numbers (the data
is mock and the answers are deterministic). Screenshots are named `NN-step.png` in the run's
output directory (§7).

**In one paragraph.** The AI button opens a full-frame **Assistant chat page** over everything —
not a sheet, no detent changes, the search sheet untouched beneath it — revealed as a circle that
grows from the button in 0.46 s while a spinning "puck" flies to the small ring in the page's
header. A typed request is answered **locally, by a rule list** (`chatIntent`: ten regexes tried in
order, a canned sentence each, and a list of place slugs), after a fixed 700 ms "Thinking" bubble.
There is no API, no fetch, no model, and no speech input — the microphone button types one of four
scripted phrases into the field, one word every 190 ms. The answer's places are shown **as result
cards inside the chat**; the chat **never alters the sheet's list or the map's pins**, and the
filter tags it computes are never displayed or applied. Tapping a card closes the chat at once and
opens the place card at half. The Filters button does not exist anywhere in the AI flow; in the
normal flow it appears beside the AI button once a query or a category is set and opens a
full-frame Filters page whose 81 chips do narrow the list, the pins and its "Show N results"
button — though only four of those 81 labels match the tag table, so most yield zero results.

## 1. What the AI button opens

The trigger is the 48 circle at x 338 in the search row (`y+21` of the sheet, so (338, 720) at
min and (338, 73) at full), `aria-label="AI search"`, `onClick: openChat`. It is a wrap of two
spans: the **ring**, inset 0, `conic-gradient(from 0deg, #FF3B6B, #FFB020, #33D17A, #22B8CF,
#4C6FFF, #B14CFF, #FF3B6B)`, and the **core**, inset 2.5 — a 43 white disc with the 16 sparkles
icon in `#17191C` — so the visible band is 2.5. The ring rotates (`aiSpin 3.6s linear infinite`,
`to { rotate(360deg) }`) and the core does not. `openChat` reads the button's centre into
`chatOrigin` ((362, 744) at min, (362, 97) at full), sets `mChatOpen`, closes the level list, seeds
`chatMsgs` with the greeting if the history is empty, and 24 ms later flips `chatReveal`; the puck
is removed 520 ms after that. Nothing about the sheet is touched: `mDetent` stayed `min` (175
tall) in A and `full` (822) in C, and `elementFromPoint` over the AI button returns the chat's
scroller while the page is up.

## 3. The requests, one at a time

Every answer comes from `chatIntent(text)`: the text is lower-cased and tried against ten regexes
in this order — `mexican|taco|burrito`, `wifi|wi-fi|internet`, `vegan|vegetarian|plant`,
`halal`, `kid|child|family`, `hungry|eat|food|lunch|dinner|breakfast`,
`coffee|caffeine|tea|drink`, `dessert|ice cream|sweet|cake`, `toilet|restroom|bathroom|wc`,
`gate|boarding|flight|depart|how long` — and the first hit returns `{ text, slugs, filters }`;
no hit returns the fallback. The tag rules (`Wi-Fi`, `Vegan`, `Halal`, `Kid-Friendly`) go through
a helper that narrows **the last answer's slugs** (`_lastSug`) by the tag table `TAGS`, or the
18-slug food list when nothing was suggested yet; that is why the first answer below says "Of
those" to a visitor who was shown nothing. The sheet's list (`results()`, 44 places at rest) and
the map's pins (the default Very Nice Fish Restaurant and Accessible restroom) were read after
every answer and **never changed**; `state.filters` stayed `[]`; no category chip was set.

So the answer to §5 of the brief is plain: the prototype **presents options in the chat** (cards,

from the chat reaches the map, and it is the ordinary `openPoi`.

## 4. The bundle, in short excerpts

- **No network, no speech.** `fetch(` appears twice: Vite's module-preload polyfill and
  `fetch(this.LIVE.tax)`, the live Pointr SDK's taxonomy file (the mock build never takes that
  path). `SpeechRecognition`, `getUserMedia`, `XMLHttpRequest`, `WebSocket`, `EventSource`: none.
  Driven, the same: over the 55 steps and four page loads the page made 122 requests — 4
  documents, 4 scripts, 8 stylesheets, 4 fonts and 102 images (60 map images from
  `pointrmapstorage.blob.core.windows.net`, 36 logos from Wikipedia and Wikimedia, 6 photos from
  picsum) and **not one of type xhr, fetch or websocket**; the only requests that followed a typed
  request were the answer cards' logos (`network.json`).
- **The answer is a timer:** `this._chT=setTimeout(()=>{const o=this.chatIntent(d);…},700)`, and
  the message pushed is `{r:"a",t:o.text,slugs:o.slugs,filters:o.filters}`.
- **The microphone is a script:** `this.DICTATION` holds four phrases ("I'm starving — …",
  "And it would be great if they have wifi", "How long would it take to get to gate 122?",
  "Where is the nearest restroom?"); `micTap` cycles them and `setInterval(…, 190)` appends one
  word at a time to `chatDraft`; a second tap runs `_micStop`. Nothing sends the draft.
- **The greeting:** `CHAT_SEED` = one assistant message, "Hello — here to help you find your
  way. Speak to me naturally."; `openChat` keeps an existing history:
  `chatMsgs:d.chatMsgs&&d.chatMsgs.length?d.chatMsgs:this.CHAT_SEED.slice()`.
- **The ring:** `"aiSpin "+(a.chatListening||a.chatThinking?"1.1s":"3.6s")` and, when listening,
  thinking or open, `",aiWave "+(a.chatThinking?"1.5s":"2.6s")+" cubic-bezier(.37,0,.63,1)"`.
  `aiRipple` is defined in the stylesheet and used nowhere.
- **The reveal:** `clipPath:"circle("+(a.chatReveal?"150%":"26px")+" at "+l.x+"px "+l.y+"px)"`
  with `transition:"clip-path .46s cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1),…,opacity .2s ease"`; the timers
  `setTimeout(()=>this.setState({chatPuck:!1}),520)},24)` and, on close,
  `setTimeout(()=>this.setState({mChatOpen:!1}),340)`.
- **The card's tap:** `this.resultRow(je,()=>{this.setState({mChatOpen:!1,chatListening:!1}),this.openPoi(je)})`.
- **The pins ignore the chat:** `mMarkers:ge?[]:(!a.mPoi&&(a.mSearchView==="results"||a.q)?j.map(w=>w.slug)…`
  — the filtered result list while searching, the open place, or the two defaults; only ten slugs
  have a position (`PINPOS`), which is why the Gates category shows no pin at all.
- **The message's filters go nowhere:** every write to `state.filters` is the Filters page's
  toggle (`filters:Ae.indexOf(P)>-1?Ae.filter(Pe=>Pe!==P):Ae.concat([P])`), its Reset, or the
  `filters:[]` clear in `startNav` / `_closeGo`; nothing reads a message's `.filters`.
- **The list the sheet draws:** `j=g.filter(w=>b.every(P=>(this.TAGS[w.slug]||[]).indexOf(P)>-1))`
  with `b=a.filters||[]` — the Filters page's choices, ANDed, over the 11-value tag table.
- **When Filters shows:** `mHideSearchTools:!!a.q||!!a.mCat`; the AI button always:
  `mShowAiInField:this.props.showAssistant??!0`.
- **Localisation:** `Assistant` and `Thinking` carry es / tr / uk / zh strings alongside `Filters`,
  `Reset`, `Recently visited`.

## 5. The Filters button, and what it opens

**In the AI flow: nowhere.** The chat page has no filter control (`filtersInside: false` in every
open step); its header is the ring, the title and Close, its foot the field, the mic and Send.
When a query or a category is set the search row's Filters button is still in the DOM under the
page, and `elementFromPoint` over its centre returns the chat page (its header title at full, its
scroller at min). The chat neither reads nor writes `filters`.

**In the normal flow** it appears with a query (typing "sta", at full: `(280, 73, 48, 48)`) or a
category (the Gates tile, at min: `(280, 720, 48, 48)`), always beside the AI button at x 338 —
the four forms of the search row in the sheet report's §4. It is 48 × 48, white, 1 px `#DEE2E6`,
radius 16, a 16 sliders icon `#17191C`; with filters applied it tints to
`color-mix(theme-500 10%)` (computed `oklch(0.953 0.023 262)`), the border `#135BEC`, the icon
`#0D44C2`, and an **18 dot** `#135BEC` with a 2 px white border sits at `(313, 70)` on its
top-right corner — a dot with no count (the JSX renders the badge span without children, although
a `mFilterCount` exists). Tapping it toggles `mFilterOpen`.

## 6. What the prototype does not do

- Call anything: no API endpoint, no request payload, no model — the answers are ten regexes and
  eleven canned sentences in the bundle; the only fetch is the live SDK's taxonomy file.
- Listen: the mic never asks for a microphone; it types one of four fixed phrases and stops.
- Alter the results: the sheet's list, the category chip, `q`, `filters` and the pins are never
  written by the chat; the `filters` it attaches to an answer are not displayed as chips and not
  applied. There is no fade or subset of pins from the chat (pin opacity read 1 throughout).
- Understand a place type it has no rule for: "charger" gets the fallback although a "charging"
  place exists; "wheelchair" is ignored because there is no accessibility rule, though six
  places are tagged `Wheelchair`; "near gate B" is dropped because the coffee rule wins.
- Present choices as chips, a list, or a category: cards only, up to 11, in the list's order.
- Focus the field on open (no keyboard would rise), scroll to the latest message on reopen, or
  let Enter insert a newline.
- Animate out when a card is tapped (the page unmounts at once), or offer the AI button on the
  place card.
- Animate the Filters page in: its `mSheetUp` keyframes are missing.
- Count on the Filters badge: the dot is empty.
- Use `aiRipple` (defined, unused) or the `mFilterChips` row (behind `never`).

## 7. Reproduce it

```
node scripts/measure-prototype-ai.cjs <out-dir>
```

From the repo root (Playwright comes from the repo's own install); `PROTOTYPE_URL` overrides the
prototype. The script writes `states.jsonl` (one line per step: the state subset, `results` and
`filteredResults` slugs, `sheetRows`, `pins`, `buttons`, the `ai` ring and the animations really
running on it, the `@keyframes` rules the document has, `filtersButton`, the `chat` page or the
`filtersPage` with every box and computed style quoted above), `bundle.json` (the fetched
bundle's `chatIntent`, the rule order, the canned texts, `CHAT_SEED`, `DICTATION`, `TAGS`,
`FILTERS`, `FILTERSEGS`, `PINPOS`, the style and timer excerpts, the keyframes, and the network
and speech counts), `network.json` (every request the page made after the load, tagged with the
step it followed, by origin, with the ones that were neither assets nor images listed apart), and
a PNG of the frame per step. The steps: **A** the four requests and the follow-ups, the
Starbucks card, the place card, close, reopen (`01`–`30`); **B** the scripted Voice input, sent,
restarted and stopped (`31`–`39`); **C** "sta", the Filters page, Vegan, Wheelchair Friendly,
Reset, Apply, the chat over the query, "coffee", Reset again (`40`–`51`); **D** the Gates tile,
the chat over the category, Shift+Enter, close (`52`–`55`). The run this report reads is
`proto-ai/run4` in the session's scratchpad; a run takes about three minutes. To re-read the
rules without driving anything, `bundle.json` is enough.
